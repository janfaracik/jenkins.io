image:/images/post-images/2026/10/06/new-manage-jenkins.png[A new way to Manage Jenkins]

With Jenkins 2.585, the new Manage Jenkins experience is enabled by default for everyone. What started as an experimental opt-in layout is now the way to configure, maintain, and secure your Jenkins controller,
as part of the wider effort to modernize the Jenkins interface with a focus on clarity and simpler navigation.

---

=== So what's new?

**Goodbye hub page**

The old Manage Jenkins 'hub' page, with its long grid of links, has been removed.
Instead, you land directly in the settings you need.

**A sidebar for every page**

Every Manage Jenkins page now has a sidebar, making it easy to jump between pages
without returning to an overview page first.

**Plugins are one click away**

Plugin pages are now simpler, and they're one click away from any Manage Jenkins page.

**Just 'Save'**

The 'Save' and 'Apply' buttons have been simplified to a single 'Save' button, which keeps you on the same page
so you can carry on where you left off.

**Plugins are already on board**

Popular plugins have already been updated for the new design, including
link:/blog/2026/04/02/redesigned-credentials-plugin/[Credentials] and
https://plugins.jenkins.io/matrix-auth/[Matrix Authorization Strategy].
If you maintain a plugin, now is a great time to check how it looks in the new layout.

---

=== Available now 🚀

The new Manage Jenkins experience is available now in Jenkins 2.585.

This option will be removed in a future release, so please let us know about anything that's holding you back.

Known open issues are tracked in link:https://github.com/jenkinsci/sig-ux/issues/10[jenkinsci/sig-ux#10].
